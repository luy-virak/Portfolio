# Design System: Virak's World — Interactive 3D Walkable Environment

> Extracted directly from Stitch Project `17918615460550967129` (*Interactive 3D Walkable Environment*) and **Screen 1 (`fc612e35423a4a14a447b3b036847a8a`: Virak's World - Interactive Diorama with Work & Contact Markers)**.

## 1. Visual Identity & Aesthetic Direction

**Terrarium Portfolio Engine** blends a **sunlit miniature floating island diorama** with **handcrafted torn-paper HUD controls** and **crisp game telemetry**:

- **Craft Paper Tactility:** Torn-edge parchment buttons (`.torn-paper`), warm walnut contact shadows (`.craft-shadow`), golden-kraft embossed display lettering (`.title-stroke`), and mechanical beveled keyboard caps (`.wasd-key`).
- **Serene Miniature Diorama:** A circular floating island (`radius: 22m`) set in soft sky-blue mist (`#DCEEF5`), populated with layered pine trees, cobblestone paths, fluffy drifting clouds, a playable terracotta-coated character, and architectural landmark pavilions.
- **Spatial Diegetic Wayfinding:** Billboarded parchment distance markers (`My Work` and `Contact`) floating above 3D buildings with live meter readouts (`14.2m`) and low-poly terracotta diamond pins.

### 4.2 Spatial HUD & Overlay Layout Rules

1. **Fullscreen 3D Viewport (`z-index: 0`):** `100vw × 100vh` fixed Three.js / React Three Fiber canvas (`#DCEEF5` sky mist, `overflow: hidden`, `select-none`).
2. **Perimeter HUD Shell (`z-index: 10`, `pointer-events-none`):**
   - Outer padding: `p-6` (`1.5rem`) on mobile, `sm:p-10` (`2.5rem`) on desktop; `flex flex-col justify-between`.
   - **Top Center Header:** `"Virak’s World"` `.title-stroke` heading + `"Explore World >"` `.torn-paper` button (`pointer-events-auto`).
   - **Bottom-Left Control Hint (`hidden sm:flex`):** `.wasd-badge` containing four `24×24px` `.wasd-key` caps (`W`, `A`, `S`, `D`) + `"MOVE"` label (reactive to live keypresses).
   - **Bottom-Center Waypoint Dock (`pointer-events-auto`):** Three `.torn-paper` buttons — `"About Me >"`, `"My Work >"`, `"Contact >"` — walking the character to the respective landmark and/or opening its overlay.
3. **Interactive 3D Distance Markers (`z-index: 5` / Diegetic 3D):**
   - Placed above **My Work** `(11.5, 7.0, -2.5)` and **Contact** `(3.5, 7.4, 12.8)` with gentle vertical bobbing, live meter distance from the character (`Space Mono` / `Epilogue` tabular readout), and click-to-inspect interaction.
4. **Overlay Screens (`z-index: 30`):**
   - **Work Showcase Modal:** Centered warm-parchment craft modal (`#FAF3E4` / `#F7EEDF`, kraft border `#DFCBB4`, `.craft-shadow`) over a soft blurred diorama view, featuring project cards, tech badges, live demo / source triggers in `.torn-paper` terracotta (`#D3543A`) & parchment (`#FCEECF`) styles.
   - **About & Skills HUD Card:** Floating parchment craft HUD card anchored over the West *About* Archive pavilion view, presenting Virak's creative technologist bio, avatar/stats badge, and categorized skill/stack keycap chips.
   - **Contact Drawer:** Right-anchored slide-over parchment craft drawer (`#FAF3E4`, border-left `#DFCBB4`) triggered by the *Contact* Telegraph Outpost marker, featuring direct social/email links and debossed linen (`#EBE4D5`) message form fields with a tactile `"Send Dispatch >"` CTA.
